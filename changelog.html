Changelog

0.1.3 — piano roll

2026-09-03

  • Full A0–C8 range with octave lines; PageUp/PageDown scroll by octave.
  • Horizontal zoom (Ctrl+wheel or toolbar) and follow-playhead.
  • Velocity lane: drag bars, multi-select moves together, paint with Ctrl.
  • Notes sound when clicked or dragged; resize many notes at once.
  • MIDI keyboard input: step entry when stopped, live record when playing.

0.1.2 — installer and updates

2026-09-03

  • Branded installer with license page; launches the app when done.
  • .bfp project files open in BeatForge on double-click; one instance only.
  • Uninstaller asks before deleting settings; projects are never touched.
  • Updates install silently and relaunch. The app checks once at launch and shows a notice.
  • One-line terminal install: irm https://repousiosjim.github.io/beatforge-site/install.ps1 | iex

0.1.0 — first public build

2026

  • Step sequencer with channel rack, and a piano roll with quantize, humanize, and chord entry.
  • Playlist/arrangement view mixing pattern and audio clips on one timeline.
  • Full mixer: EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, chorus, distortion, multiband, sends and buses.
  • Automation lanes for any parameter, plus audio recording and non-destructive WAV import (trim, split, fade, gain).
  • WAV, MP3, and MIDI export with live/export parity.
  • AI assistant for drum patterns, melodies, mix suggestions and sample picking, on your own OpenRouter key, with per-feature model choice, cost tracking and budgets.
  • Experimental VST3 instrument scanning and audition on Windows.
  • Forge Dark and Forge Studio themes, and autosave with three-slot recovery.

Auto-update: the app checks this site's releases for new versions.
